Features

Everything the paid removers do — offline

Drop in one photo or a hundred. Everything runs on your PC — your images never leave it.

AI on your PC

Two AI engines built in. On PCs with a graphics card or plenty of RAM, Lasso picks the sharpest one for hair and fur automatically.

Batch mode

Drop a whole folder. Lasso works through the queue and exports them all in one go.

Right-click any image

Choose "Remove background" in File Explorer — the cut-out is saved right next to the original. No app to open.

Touch-up brush

Erase leftovers or restore anything the AI missed, with soft edges and unlimited undo.

New backgrounds

Transparent, solid colours, gradients, a blurred version of the original, or your own image.

Shadows & stickers

Add a soft drop shadow or a sticker-style outline, auto-crop to the subject, and export PNG, WebP or JPG.

Install

Up and running in a minute

Three steps. No account needed.

Download

Click Download for Windows above. You'll get Lasso-Setup.exe.

Run the installer

Open it and click through the setup — pick a folder or keep the default. Lasso opens when it's done.

Cut it out

On first launch Lasso downloads its AI once. Then drop in images — or right-click one — and export.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info then Run anyway. That appears for new apps from independent developers that aren't code-signed yet.
FAQ

Questions

Is Lasso really free?

Yes — no account, no credits, no watermark and no limit on how many images you process.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. The AI runs on your own PC. The only download is the AI itself, once, the first time you open Lasso. After that it works with no internet at all.

How fast is it?

Usually 1–3 seconds per image. On PCs with a graphics card or 16 GB+ of RAM, Best quality uses a sharper engine for hair and fur, which can take a few seconds longer.

Where's the right-click option on Windows 11?

Right-click an image, choose Show more options, then Remove background. The result is saved next to the original as "name (no bg).png".

Does it work on Mac?

Right now Lasso is Windows 10 and 11 only.
